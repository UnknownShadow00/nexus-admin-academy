# Quiz 24-Week Placement Plan

Audit date: 2026-07-19 UTC

## Placement principles

- The current certification-order weeks are not treated as curriculum truth.
- One weekly assessment is required; practice quizzes stay short and optional/low-stakes. Remediation appears only when a learner needs it.
- Endpoint assessments at Weeks 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, and 24 may satisfy the weekly requirement and cumulative/gate role at once, avoiding duplicate workload.
- Certification banks are removed from the normal checklist and placed in a separate optional library.
- No imported quiz becomes required until every answer is independently verified and explanations are added.
- A quiz covering several prior weeks is placed at the review endpoint rather than forced under a single lesson.

## Current versus proposed workload

Today, Weeks 1–9 contain 90 quizzes / 863 questions, while Weeks 10–23 contain only 14 quizzes / 104 questions and Week 24 has none. More precisely, the database distribution is 6/10/14/3/7/16/15/4/15 quizzes in Weeks 1–9. This is a certification-import artifact, not a teachable workload.

The proposed normal checklist is generally 7–28 questions per week, with remediation adding 5–8 only when needed. The optional certification library is outside weekly completion/progress. Five students and one mentor can review one required score, selected practice, and practical evidence without processing 104 required completions.

## Promotion and cumulative design

- Week 4: expand #5 to sample ticket quality, hardware/Windows triage, priority, escalation, and communication; keep Multi-Ticket Simulation 1 as the practical authority.
- Week 8: expand #9 across Weeks 5–8; keep Multi-Ticket Simulation 2 as the practical authority.
- Week 12: expand #14 across subnetting, VLAN/access/trunk, routing/services, secure administration, and troubleshooting; CLI completion and ticket evidence remain the promotion proof.
- Week 16: expand #18 across AD, joins/file access, GPO, DNS/DHCP, and PowerShell; this is a cumulative review, not Gate 4 by itself.
- Week 17: #19 is the Gate 4 knowledge check; the real restore ticket is non-negotiable practical evidence.
- Week 20: expand #22 across Linux permissions, SSH, systemd/logs, networking/DNS, cron, web/firewall, backup, monitoring.
- Week 24: move #25 from Week 23 and expand it to 15–20 scenario questions spanning Windows, Linux, networking, cloud, security escalation, communication, and verification. The Maple & Finch capstone remains Gate 5's primary evidence.

## Platform behavior and required product changes

### What students see now

- `GET /api/quizzes` filters to `status == published`; a `week_number` query additionally filters by week. With no week filter it returns every published quiz, with no pagination.
- The student Week Plan queries every published quiz whose `week_number` equals the current week. Therefore **all published quizzes assigned to a week appear in “This Week”**, regardless of `lesson_id`.
- `lesson_id` is returned as metadata and used in admin editing, but it does not place a quiz under a lesson on the student weekly page or quiz library.
- Unlinked quizzes do appear: all 79 imported quizzes are unlinked, published, and therefore visible by week and in the all-weeks library.
- `status=draft` hides a quiz from list/detail/week-plan routes. There are currently no drafts.
- The standalone Quizzes page has an “All Weeks” toggle and therefore functions as a complete published library. It has no required/optional/certification distinction.
- The week-plan progress denominator includes every published quiz in that week. The imported overload therefore behaves as required work in progress calculations even though no explicit required flag exists.

### Admin visibility

- `/api/admin/quizzes` orders newest-first and hard-limits results to 50.
- The Module Manager labels the result “Recent Quizzes” and supplies no pagination, total, week filter, status filter, or full-library route. It cannot display all 104 at once; 54 are unreachable from that list.
