# Identity, Access & Security Controls

## 1. What is this?
Recognize physical and logical controls, least privilege, Zero Trust, MFA, ACLs, SSO/SAML, JIT/PAM, IAM, MDM, and DLP at an A+ support level.

## 2. Why does an IT worker care?
Security tickets often look like ordinary access or application problems. The technician must restore approved work without weakening controls, granting unnecessary privilege, or destroying useful evidence.

## 3. Watch / Read
Complete the required resource **resource.aplus.m13.logical_security** before the Quick Check. Use optional references only when you need another explanation or a vendor procedure.

## 4. What you actually need to remember
- Physical controls include door locks, badges/key fobs/smart cards, biometrics, guards/cameras, fences/lighting, cable locks, and access control vestibules. Know what they protect; do not overfocus on product trivia.
- Physical access authentication and logical account authorization solve different problems and should reinforce each other.
- Least privilege means giving only the access needed for the job—not the easiest access to grant.
- Zero Trust means access is continuously evaluated; being 'inside the network' is not automatically trusted.
- MFA combines different factor types. Two passwords are not two factors.
- ACLs express who or what is allowed or denied access to a resource.
- SSO reduces repeated sign-ins; SAML commonly carries web authentication assertions between an identity provider and a service.
- JIT/PAM limits privileged access by time, approval, or task instead of leaving privilege permanently assigned.
- IAM manages identities and access; MDM manages mobile endpoints; DLP helps prevent sensitive data from leaving approved boundaries.

## 5. At work
- When a user asks for access, verify the requested resource and business need before changing permissions.
- Do not bypass an approval or privileged-access workflow just because a user is blocked.
- Physical access controls and logical controls often reinforce each other; a badge does not replace account authorization.

## 6. Commands / Tools
- Directory/IAM portal or approved directory console
- Ticket/approval record
- Access-control or group-membership evidence

## 7. Interview / Explain
Explain least privilege and give one example of a safer alternative to permanent administrator access.
