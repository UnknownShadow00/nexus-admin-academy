## 1. What is this?

The CPU executes instructions and must match the motherboard socket and
firmware support list. A heatsink, fan, and correctly applied thermal compound
move heat away so the processor does not throttle or shut down.

## 2. Why does an IT worker care?

Wrong sockets can damage parts; poor cooling causes instability that may look
like a software problem.

## 3. Watch / read

- Professor Messer — CPU Features and Cooling (220-1201). *(required)*

## 4. What you need to remember

- Socket shape alone does not prove firmware/chipset support.
- Align the marked corner; never force a CPU.
- Clean old compound and apply new compound in the amount/pattern specified by
  the CPU or cooler manufacturer when reseating a cooler.
- Connect the fan to the CPU-fan header and verify temperature/fan readings.

## 5. Real workplace example

A PC powers off during heavy work. The CPU fan cable is disconnected. The
technician reconnects it, monitors temperatures under load, and documents the test.

## 6. Commands / tools

Use vendor firmware diagnostics, hardware-monitor readings, and a controlled
load test. Power off and disconnect mains power before internal work.

## 7. Interview / example

**Why can a cooling fault resemble an application fault?** Heat rises under
load, producing throttling, crashes, or shutdowns when demanding apps run.
