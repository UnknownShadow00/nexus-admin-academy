## 1. What is this?

A safe upgrade is a controlled change: identify the need, confirm compatibility,
protect data and hardware, record the original state, install, test, and retain
a rollback path.

## 2. Why does an IT worker care?

Good parts can still produce downtime when prerequisites, ESD, cables, firmware,
or verification are missed.

## 3. Watch / read

- The target system's manufacturer service manual. *(required)*

## 4. What you need to remember

- Back up important data and obtain approval before change.
- Shut down, unplug, discharge, use ESD controls, photograph/label connections.
- Change one logical thing at a time when diagnosing.
- Verify POST, device recognition, temperatures, drivers, and the original task.
- Document part/serial, reason, result, and rollback/escalation.

## 5. Real workplace example

After a network-card replacement, link light alone is insufficient. The tech
checks Device Manager, obtains an address, reaches the gateway, and records it.

## 6. Commands / tools

Checklist, service manual, asset system, ESD controls, firmware diagnostics,
Device Manager, and the application or workload that originally failed.

## 7. Interview / example

**What makes an upgrade complete?** The component is safely installed and
recognised, the original requirement works, no new issue appears, and the change is documented.
